# Letyar Voice

Letyar sounds concise, precise, calm, capable, and honest.

## Prefer

- Clear over complicated
- Useful over decorative
- Crafted over rushed
- Practical over theoretical
- Shipped over unfinished

## Avoid

- Empty buzzwords
- Inflated claims
- Generic agency language
- Unnecessary jargon
- Fake urgency

## Core message

**Every build carries a fingerprint.**
