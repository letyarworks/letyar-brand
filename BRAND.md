# Letyar Brand Foundation

## Name

**Letyar** — from Myanmar **လက်ရာ**.

The name carries two connected meanings:

1. Workmanship / craftsmanship
2. Fingerprint — the maker's distinctive mark

The central idea is:

> **Every build carries a fingerprint.**

## Positioning

Letyar is a maker-focused web, software, and product engineering brand. It builds practical digital products, websites, interfaces, systems, and tools with attention to the details that make the work recognisably its own.

Letyar is **not positioned as a generic agency**.

## Taglines

**Primary:** Every build carries a fingerprint.

**Alternative:** Built by hand, shipped on time.

**Myanmar:** ကိုယ်ပိုင်လက်ရာနဲ့ တည်ဆောက်ပါတယ်။

## Pronunciation & spelling

- Pronunciation: **LET-yar**
- Always write: **Letyar**
- Logo treatment may use: `letyar.`
- Do not write: `Let Yar`, `Let-Yar`, `LetYar`, or `Letyaa`

## Design principles

- Crafted, not decorated
- Precise, not noisy
- Practical, not generic
- Distinctive, not trendy for its own sake
- Human-made, technically capable

## Visual direction

The **Yun Ridge** mark references Myanmar craftsmanship and carving lines while forming an L-like fingerprint / rising path structure. The warm-gold point acts as the maker's signature.

## Typography

- General Sans 600 — display / headings
- Satoshi 400 / 500 — body
- JetBrains Mono 400 — code / labels
- Noto Sans Myanmar 400 / 600 — Myanmar text

## Voice

Letyar should sound concise, precise, calm, capable, and honest.

Avoid buzzwords, inflated claims, generic agency language, and unnecessary jargon.
