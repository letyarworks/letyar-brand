# Letyar

> **Every build carries a fingerprint.**

Letyar (လက်ရာ) is a web and product brand built around the idea that good digital work should feel intentional, crafted, and recognisable.

We build practical digital products, websites, interfaces, and tools with attention to the details that give every build its own fingerprint.

## Brand

**Name:** Letyar / လက်ရာ  
**Meaning:** Workmanship, craftsmanship, a maker's distinctive touch  
**Core idea:** Every build carries a fingerprint.  
**Primary tagline:** Every build carries a fingerprint.  
**Alternative tagline:** Built by hand, shipped on time.

## What We Do

- 🌐 Web experiences
- 📱 Digital products
- 🧩 Product interfaces and UI systems
- ⚙️ Practical tools and utilities
- 🤖 AI-assisted product development
- 🛠️ Custom digital builds

## Philosophy

Letyar is built on a simple principle:

> **Craft matters.**

Technology is the material. Design is the structure. Engineering is the craft. The final product should be useful, clear, reliable, and recognisably its own.

We prefer:

- Clear over complicated
- Useful over decorative
- Crafted over rushed
- Practical over theoretical
- Shipped over unfinished

## Brand Direction

Letyar is not positioned as a generic agency. It is a maker-focused technology brand for building and shipping digital products with a distinct identity.

Every project should leave a trace of its maker — a **fingerprint**.
